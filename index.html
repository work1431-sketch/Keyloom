Keyloom

Keyword research for handmade sellers

Keyloom is a private research tool that helps an independent seller choose better keywords, tags and titles for their own products, and see when to list for each season.

What Keyloom does

Keyword estimates

Compares phrases by estimated demand and competition, so the owner can pick the ones worth using.

Tag and title ideas

Suggests up to 13 tags and a title within Etsy's length limits, based on the phrases it scored.

Listing checks

Reviews a listing's title, tags, photos and description against simple best practices.

Seasons and calendar

Shows when shopper interest peaks and when to have listings ready.

Public shop research

Looks at public shop and listing information to understand a niche.

Profit calculator

Works out what is left from a sale after fees, materials and shipping.

How Keyloom uses Etsy data

  • It reads public listing and shop information through the official Etsy Open API.
  • It does not read private account data, orders, messages or buyer details.
  • It does not create or edit listings, and it does not send email.
  • It does not scrape Etsy web pages.
  • Keyword suggestions and seasonal trends come from Google's public autocomplete and Google Trends.
  • Research results are stored on the owner's own computer.

Who it is for

Keyloom is built for personal use by one seller. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up. This page exists to describe the tool.
